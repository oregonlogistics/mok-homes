Meet the designer

Margareta
“Marga” Stefiuc.

With deep roots in Chicago’s North Shore, Margareta “Marga” Stefiuc brings local insight and a rare blend of real estate experience and interior design expertise to every client relationship.

After studying interior design at the Art Institute of Chicago, Marga became a professional member of the American Society of Interior Designers. Her education and training in the United States and Europe, experience as a property investor, and real estate license give her a thoughtful perspective on buying, selling, and making a home your own.

Outside the studio, Marga enjoys time with her husband and three children, gardening, planning celebrations, and training for endurance races. She has completed two full distance Ironman races, four half distance races, and several marathons. Her dedication to training reflects the focus and commitment she brings to her clients.

Languages: Romanian and Hungarian
